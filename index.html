01 About

Computation first, biology always.

I am in the final year of a B.Tech in Bioinformatics at Marwadi University (NAAC A+). Since August 2026 I am working as a Data Analyst on the core R&D team at Elmentoz Research in Hyderabad, building and testing genomics pipelines.

The through-line in my work is applying predictive modelling to data that was generated to answer a biological or clinical question. In practice that has meant two complementary tracks. On the machine learning side, I have built classifiers and deep learning frameworks on oncology, neurological, hematological and endocrine datasets — ensemble models for breast cancer outcome prediction, a hybrid pipeline for Parkinson's disease diagnosis from clinical and voice features, a first-author classification study on alpha thalassemia variants, a noise-tolerant ensemble for hypothyroid classification, and PyTorch models for automated sleep apnea detection from physiological signals.

I have also contributed to a review of conventional and bioinformatics-led approaches to norovirus detection and surveillance, published in Decoding Infection and Transmission — work that sits closer to pathogen genomics than to the clinical modelling above, and that I would like to do more of.

On the genomics side, I work in Python-based bioinformatics pipelines: quality control, clustering, differential expression and cell-type annotation for single-cell RNA-seq; and differential gene expression, functional enrichment, pathway visualisation and protein–protein interaction network analysis for bulk cancer transcriptomes. My current minor research project uses the TCGA-LIHC cohort to look for prognostic biomarkers and candidate therapeutic targets in hepatocellular carcinoma.

A Summer Research Fellowship at IIEST Shibpur in 2025 sat at the join between those two tracks: designing and training PyTorch models on high-dimensional physiological signal data, with preprocessing, feature extraction and evaluation pipelines that carry over directly to large-scale sequence analysis.

I care about work being reproducible as much as accurate — end-to-end workflows, documented parameters, and results that hold up when someone else runs them. I am looking for research and applied roles where machine learning is used to answer genomics, drug-target and biomedical questions.

P01
Minor research project 2026 – 2027 In progress

Integrated Bioinformatics Analysis of Hepatocellular Carcinoma

Identifying prognostic biomarkers and candidate therapeutic targets in liver cancer from the TCGA-LIHC cohort, by chaining differential expression, functional enrichment, pathway visualisation, network analysis and drug–gene mapping into one reproducible analysis.

  • DESeq2
  • g:Profiler
  • Pathview
  • STRING
  • Cytoscape
  • DrugBank
  • R
  • Python
Read the full write-up
The problem

Hepatocellular carcinoma is diagnosed late and has few reliable prognostic markers. Public tumour–normal transcriptome cohorts such as TCGA-LIHC hold enough signal to nominate candidate biomarkers and druggable targets, but only if expression, pathway and interaction evidence are read together rather than in isolation.

My contribution

Designing and running the full analysis: cohort preparation, the differential expression model, the enrichment and pathway layers, construction and interrogation of the protein–protein interaction network, and the final drug–gene mapping step.

P02
Academic project April 2026 Completed

Single-Cell Transcriptomic Analysis: Differential Expression & Cell-Type Annotation

Reproducible, end-to-end Python workflows for scRNA-seq — quality control through clustering, differential expression and cell-type annotation — applied to public datasets to investigate disease-associated transcriptional changes and surface candidate biomarkers.

  • Python
  • Scanpy
  • NumPy
  • Pandas
  • GEO / SRA datasets
Read the full write-up
The problem

Single-cell datasets resolve transcriptional change to individual cell types, but the analysis path from raw counts to annotated populations involves many consequential parameter choices. Without a workflow that records those choices, results are hard to reproduce or compare across datasets.

My contribution

Engineered the workflows end to end in Python, structured so that quality-control thresholds, clustering resolution and annotation decisions are explicit and re-runnable on a new dataset.

Sleep Apnea Detection & CPAP Therapy Optimization via Deep Learning

06 Research interests

Prognostic biomarker discovery and therapeutic target nomination from tumour transcriptomes, building on the TCGA-LIHC analysis.
